vNext Protected Backups

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes the personal vNext Protected Backups utility and this informational website.

Data accessed and its purpose

The utility processes the owner's selected file contents, names, metadata, and verification records to create, verify, and restore backups. Google Drive access uses the drive.file permission for files created by the app or explicitly authorized for it.

Where data is stored

Selected originals, backup copies, and verification records may be stored on the owner's computer, external storage, and private Google Drive storage. Readable backup copies are kept separately from encrypted snapshots; not every copy is encrypted by this utility.

Google authorization credentials are kept in restricted local storage outside ordinary backup packages. Recovery keys are kept separately from those packages. These credentials and keys are not published on this website.

Use and sharing

The utility uses data for the owner's backup, integrity-checking, and recovery operations. It does not sell data, use it for advertising, publish backup contents, or automatically share backups with unrelated parties. Google hosts and processes the Drive copies as the storage provider under its own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and the owner's controls

Backup copies are retained until the owner deliberately removes them. The protected workflow does not automatically delete or retire backups. The owner can stop the utility or revoke its Google authorization through their Google Account. Revoking access stops future authorized operations; it does not delete copies already retained locally or in Drive.

This website

These pages contain no signup or upload form, analytics code, tracking scripts, or third-party assets. They do not provide access to backups. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub operates the hosting service and may collect technical visit information, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy practices.

Policy changes

The policy will be updated if the utility's data practices change. Its personal-use scope and backup-sharing settings are separate from whether these informational pages are publicly accessible.
