Personal backup and recovery utility

vNext Protected Backups

vNext Protected Backups helps its owner preserve selected files and recover them when needed. It creates backup copies, checks their contents, and supports restoration to separate destinations.

How it uses Google Drive

The utility uses the owner's Google authorization to create and read backup files in Google Drive. Its access is limited to files created by the app or explicitly authorized for it. It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission.

Backup material may be held on the owner's computer, an external drive, and private Google Drive storage. Readable copies and separately encrypted backup snapshots serve different recovery needs. The utility does not make these backups public.

Who it is for

This is a personal utility, not a public backup service. This website provides information only; it has no signup, upload form, or access to backup contents.
